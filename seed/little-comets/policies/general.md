# About Little Comets

Little Comets is a small private preschool for ages 2 to 5, owned and directed by Nadia Castellanos. We have three rooms: Moon (ages 2-3), Star (ages 3-4) and Rocket (ages 4-5), with a teacher-to-child ratio of about 1 to 8.

## Our approach
Play-based learning with a space theme: each month we explore a topic such as the moon, planets or rockets. Rocket Room children work on kindergarten readiness (letters, counting, writing their names).

## Communication
- The parent portal posts photos and daily notes.
- Parent-teacher conferences are in November and April.
- A monthly newsletter goes out by email on the first Friday.

## Safety and visitors
The door is locked during the day. Visitors sign in at the office. Please park in the lot and not on Orbit Lane in front of the neighbors' houses.

## Guidance
Teachers use calm reminders, choices and short breaks. We do not use punishment such as time-outs longer than a few minutes.
