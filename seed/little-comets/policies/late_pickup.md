# Late Pickup

Please collect your child by the end of their program: **{{fact:part_day_end}}** (part-day), **{{fact:full_day_end}}** (full-day), or **{{hours:weekdays.close}}** (extended care).

## Grace period and fee
- Grace period: **{{fact:late_pickup_grace}}**. No fee during the grace period.
- After that, the fee is **{{fee:late_pickup}} {{fee:late_pickup.period}}**, or part of one. For example, one minute past the grace period counts as one full block, and each further block adds the fee again.
- Late fees are added to your Friday weekly payment.

## Extended care
If you are picking up at the end of extended care, the grace period runs from closing ({{hours:weekdays.close}}). Fees start with the first block after that.

## Wednesdays
The center closes at {{hours:wednesday.close}} on Wednesdays, so the grace period runs from then that day.

## After 6:00 PM
If we haven't heard from you by 6:00 PM, we will call your emergency contacts. Please call or text the front desk at {{contact:front_desk.phone}} if you are delayed.

## Who may pick up
Only adults on your authorized list, with photo ID. Please tell us in advance by message if someone new is coming.
