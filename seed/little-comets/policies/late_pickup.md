# Late Pickup

Please collect your child by the end of their program: **12:30 PM** (part-day), **3:30 PM** (full-day), or **5:30 PM** (extended care).

## Grace period and fee
- There is a **10-minute grace period** after your program ends. No fee during the grace period.
- After that, the fee is **$15 for every 15-minute block**, or part of one. For example, 11 to 25 minutes late is $15, and 26 to 40 minutes late is $30.
- Late fees are added to your Friday weekly payment.

## Extended care
If you are picking up at the end of extended care, the grace period ends at **5:40 PM**. Fees start with the first block after that.

## Wednesdays
The center closes at 3:30 PM on Wednesdays, so late pickup counts from 3:40 PM that day.

## After 6:00 PM
If we haven't heard from you by 6:00 PM, we will call your emergency contacts. Please call or text the front desk at (505) 555-0160 if you are delayed.

## Who may pick up
Only adults on your authorized list, with photo ID. Please tell us in advance by message if someone new is coming.
