# Tuition and Payments

- Tuition is **weekly**, paid every **Friday** for the following week.
- Pay by card or bank transfer through the parent portal. We do not accept cash or checks.
- You pay for a week even if your child is out sick or on vacation, and for weeks when we close for a holiday.
- **Returned payment fee: $25**, plus any bank charge.
- **Sibling discount:** $10 per week off for each additional sibling.
- If a payment is more than **two weeks** late, your child's place may be released.
- Changing between part-day and full-day, or between 3 and 5 days, requires two weeks' notice.
