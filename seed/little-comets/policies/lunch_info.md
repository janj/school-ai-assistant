# Lunch, Snacks and Allergies

## Who provides what
- **Families pack lunch** for full-day children. Lunch is at 12:30 PM.
- **The center provides a morning snack** (9:30 AM) for every child, and water at all times.
- Part-day children go home before lunch and do not need to bring one.
- There is no breakfast or afternoon snack. Extended-care children may bring a snack for their own use.

## Packed lunch tips
- Label the lunchbox and containers with your child's name.
- Include something your child can open alone. Staff will help with zippers and wrappers.
- There is **no microwave** or refrigerator for lunches, so please include an ice pack for items that need to stay cold.
- Send food that is cut into small pieces (grapes quartered, hot dogs sliced lengthwise).

## Nut-free center
The center is **completely nut-free**. Please do not send peanuts, tree nuts, peanut butter, almond butter or foods with nut ingredients. Sunflower seed butter is fine. No sweets or soda in lunches.

## Allergies
- Tell us about any allergies on the application and provide a doctor's action plan.
- Our snack list is posted at the front desk and sent monthly. The Snack Coordinator, Pilar Duarte, can answer questions at snacks@littlecomets.test.
- Allergy information is posted in each classroom.

## Outside food
Only the packed lunch your family sends, and approved birthday treats, are allowed. Children may not trade or share food.
