# Items from Home

## Please bring
- A labeled **backpack** with a full change of clothes (and spare underwear for children in potty learning).
- A **packed lunch** and water bottle on full-day days (see Lunch and Allergies).
- A rest-time **blanket** and small pillow, taken home each Friday to wash (full-day children).
- Sunscreen already applied at home; staff will reapply with a signed form.

## Show and tell
Rocket Room (ages 4-5) has show-and-tell on **Fridays**. Star and Moon Rooms have a "special item" day once a month. Items should be small, safe and not expensive.

## Allowed
- A comfort toy or stuffed animal for rest time
- Books for sharing
- Rain boots and a rain jacket, which stay in the cubby

## Not allowed
- Toy weapons, video games, tablets or phones
- Candy, gum, soda or juice boxes
- Sharp objects, small loose items (coins, marbles, beads)
- Medication in the backpack. Hand it to the director with a signed form.
- Peanuts, tree nuts or foods containing them (see Lunch and Allergies)
- Open-toed sandals; please send closed-toe shoes

We cannot be responsible for lost items, so please label everything.
