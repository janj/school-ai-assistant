# Illness Policy

With only three small rooms, one sick child can affect a lot of families. Please follow these guidelines. Our Health and Safety Officer, {{contact:health_officer}}, makes the final decision: {{contact:health_officer.phone}}.

## Keep your child home if:
- **Temperature of {{fact:fever_threshold}}**
- Vomiting at any time in the last **{{fact:stomach_bug_exclusion}}**
- Diarrhea, or 2 or more loose stools in a day
- A cough that is wet or constant, or green or yellow nasal discharge
- Red or crusty eyes
- Any unexplained rash
- Sore throat or earache
- Lice

## Return rules
- **Fever:** fever-free for **24 hours** without medicine, **and** the child is acting like themself.
- **Vomiting or diarrhea:** symptom-free for **{{fact:stomach_bug_exclusion}}**.
- **Antibiotic illness** (strep, ear infection, pink eye): after **24 hours** of treatment.
- **Contagious illness** (chickenpox, hand-foot-and-mouth, flu, COVID-19): until a doctor says the child may return, or the minimum isolation time passes.
- **Lice:** after treatment; staff check the child's hair on return.

## During the day
If a child shows symptoms, we call you. **Please pick up within {{fact:sick_pickup_window}}.** The child will rest in the office with a teacher.

## Medication
Staff give only prescription medicine that is in its original bottle with a signed authorization form. We do not give over-the-counter medicine.

Please tell us about any diagnosis so we can alert the other families.
