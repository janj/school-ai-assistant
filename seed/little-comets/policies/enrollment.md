# Enrollment

Little Comets is a small preschool with **about 36 children** in three rooms. We enroll children aged **2 to 5** (children must be 2 years old by September 1; being potty-trained is not required).

## Steps
1. **Book a tour** with {{contact:enrollment}} ({{contact:enrollment.email}}). Tours run Tuesday and Thursday mornings, 9:30 to 10:15 AM.
2. **Choose a schedule:** part-day or full-day, 3 or 5 days a week.
3. **Submit the application** and the **{{fee:registration}} registration fee** to hold a place.
4. **Complete the paperwork** and attend a 30-minute welcome visit.
5. **Start date:** most children start in late August or at the start of January.

## Documents needed
- Enrollment application and emergency contacts
- **Immunization record** (up to date for age, or a signed state exemption)
- Well-child visit form from the last 12 months
- Allergy action plan, if applicable
- Authorization to pick up form

## Immunizations
Children must have the immunizations required for their age, including MMR, DTaP, polio, varicella, and hepatitis B. A doctor-signed schedule is accepted for children catching up.

## Waitlist
- When a room is full, families join a **waitlist** by emailing {{contact:enrollment.email}} with the child's birth date and the preferred schedule. There is no fee.
- Places are offered in the order of the waitlist, with priority to siblings.
- Most openings occur in August and January.
