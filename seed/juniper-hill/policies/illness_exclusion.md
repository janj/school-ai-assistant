# Illness and When to Keep Your Child Home

Rosalind Vega, RN, the center nurse, makes the final call on whether a child may stay at or return to the center. Questions: (505) 555-0124.

## Keep your child home or we will call you to pick up if:
- **Fever of 100.4 degrees F (38.0 C) or higher** (taken by mouth, ear or forehead). Children under 12 months with a fever of 100.4 F or higher must be seen by a provider.
- **Vomiting** one or more times since the previous evening
- **Diarrhea:** 2 or more loose stools in a day, or any stool with blood
- Rash with a fever or behavior change
- Pink, red or draining eyes (suspected conjunctivitis)
- Sore throat with fever, or a confirmed strep throat
- Unusual tiredness, fussiness or a persistent cough that makes it hard to take part
- Lice or nits (see below)
- Head injury or other injury that needs medical attention

## When can my child return?
- **Fever:** fever-free for **24 hours** without fever-reducing medicine (such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen).
- **Vomiting or diarrhea:** symptom-free for **24 hours**.
- **Conjunctivitis:** after 24 hours of prescribed treatment, or with a provider's note.
- **Strep throat:** after 12 hours on antibiotics, and no fever for 24 hours.
- **Rash:** a provider's note that it is not contagious.
- **Hand, foot and mouth, chickenpox, pertussis, and other reportable illnesses:** a provider's release note is required.
- **Lice:** after one treatment, and a nurse check at drop-off.

## At the center
If a child becomes ill, staff isolate them in the quiet room with an adult and call the parents. **Pick up within 45 minutes** of the call, please.

## Medication
Staff give medicine only with a signed medication form and the original labeled container. Hand it to the nurse, not the teacher.

## Notify us
Please tell the front desk the diagnosis so we can alert other families anonymously.
