# Meals, Allergies and Outside Food

## Meals are included
Juniper Hill's own kitchen, run by Food Service Manager Desmond Whitfield, prepares **breakfast, lunch and a PM snack** every day. They are included in tuition. The menu follows the federal child nutrition guidelines and is posted weekly outside each room.

## Infants
Families provide formula or breast milk in labeled bottles. The center provides baby food and iron-fortified cereal once the baby has tried each food at home first.

## Allergies and special diets
- The kitchen is **peanut-free and tree-nut-free**.
- List all food allergies on the enrollment form and give the nurse a **doctor-signed allergy action plan**.
- Allergy information is posted (without photos in public areas) in the kitchen and each classroom.
- Substitutions for medical, religious or cultural reasons are available with a written request. Please give 5 school days' notice.
- Milk substitutes (soy or oat) are available with a doctor's note.

## Outside food
- Outside food is **not allowed**, except infant bottles and baby food, and food for a documented medical diet that the nurse has approved.
- If your child will miss the breakfast window, please feed them at home; the kitchen cannot hold meals.
- Fruit, vegetables and snacks for celebrations: see the Birthdays policy.
