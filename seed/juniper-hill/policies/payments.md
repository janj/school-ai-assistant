# Tuition and Payments

- Tuition is billed **monthly**. For the due date and when a payment counts as late, see Fees (tuition due date).
- Accepted: automatic bank draft (preferred), check, or card at the front desk (a 3% card fee applies to in-person card payments).
- A **{{fee:returned_payment}} returned payment fee** applies to returned checks and declined drafts.
- Tuition does not change for holidays, closures, or absences.
- Families with siblings enrolled get the sibling discount (see Fees).
- If an account is **two months overdue**, care may be suspended until it is brought current.
- Receipts for taxes are issued each January. Tuition-assistance slots are handled by the Enrollment Coordinator.
