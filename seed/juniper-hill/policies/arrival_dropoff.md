# Arrival and Drop-off

## Arrival window
- The center opens at **{{hours:weekdays.open}}**. Children may arrive any time before **{{fact:arrival_cutoff}}**.
- Breakfast times for each room are in the daily schedule. Children who arrive after breakfast should have eaten at home.
- **Late arrival:** if your child will arrive after {{fact:arrival_cutoff}}, call or message the front desk **by {{fact:arrival_cutoff}}** so meal counts and staffing are correct. Children arriving late without notice may be asked to wait until after the morning outdoor play (see the daily schedule) to join the room smoothly.
- Please do not arrive during nap or rest time (see the daily schedule for your child's room) unless it is an emergency.

## Sign-in
- An adult must walk the child to the classroom, sign in on the tablet at the room door and hand the child directly to a teacher.
- Infant families: please label bottles, and tell the teacher about feeding and sleep since the last visit.
- Do not leave a child in the car or in a hallway, even for a moment.

## Absences
Please call or email the front desk by {{fact:arrival_cutoff}} if your child will be absent. If we haven't heard from you, we call home that morning.

## Goodbyes
Short, confident goodbyes work best. Teachers will text a photo update if your child has a hard drop-off.
