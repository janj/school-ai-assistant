# Arrival and Drop-off

## Arrival window
- The center opens at **6:30 AM**. Children may arrive any time before **9:00 AM**.
- Breakfast is served from 8:00 to 8:45 AM. Children who arrive after 8:30 AM should have eaten at home.
- **Late arrival:** children arriving after **9:30 AM** need a call or message to the front desk **before 9:00 AM** so meal counts and staffing are correct. Children arriving after 10:00 AM without notice may be asked to wait until after outdoor play (10:30 AM) to join the room smoothly.
- Please do not arrive during nap time (roughly 11:45 AM to 2:00 PM) unless it is an emergency.

## Sign-in
- An adult must walk the child to the classroom, sign in on the tablet at the room door and hand the child directly to a teacher.
- Infant families: please label bottles, and tell the teacher about feeding and sleep since the last visit.
- Do not leave a child in the car or in a hallway, even for a moment.

## Absences
Please call or email the front desk by 9:00 AM if your child will be absent. An unexplained absence by 9:30 AM triggers a call home.

## Goodbyes
Short, confident goodbyes work best. Teachers will text a photo update if your child has a hard drop-off.
