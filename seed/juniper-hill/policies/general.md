# About Juniper Hill

Juniper Hill Early Learning Center is a public-style program serving infants through pre-K, with rooms named for woodland animals: Sparrows (infants), Chipmunks (toddlers), Foxes (twos and preschool) and Owls (pre-K).

## Communication
- Daily updates and photos go through the family app. Teachers answer messages within one business day.
- Parent-teacher conferences are held twice a year, in October and April.
- An open house is held in late August.

## Parking and safety
- The front door is locked during the day; ring the bell or use your family key fob.
- Park only in marked spaces in the front lot and never leave a car running.
- Visitors sign in at the front desk and wear a badge.

## Guidance
We use positive guidance: redirection, choices and short calm-down breaks. We never use physical punishment or withhold food.

## Dress
Dress your child for active play and mess. Closed-toe shoes with backs, please.
