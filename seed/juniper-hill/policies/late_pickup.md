# Late Pickup Policy

Juniper Hill closes at **{{hours:weekdays.close}}** for pick-up ({{hours:early_release.close}} on early release days). Staff schedules and licensing ratios depend on every child leaving on time.

## Fees
- The late fee is **{{fee:late_pickup}} {{fee:late_pickup.period}}**. Grace period: {{fact:late_pickup_grace}}.
- Fees are billed on your next monthly invoice and are due with tuition.
- The time on the front office clock is the official time.

## Repeated lateness
- **3 late pickups in a calendar month:** a written notice from the Assistant Director.
- **5 late pickups in a semester (Aug-Dec or Jan-Jul):** a meeting with the Director, and your child's place may be reviewed.

## If you are running late
Call the front desk at {{contact:front_desk.phone}} as early as you can. Calling does not remove the fee, but it lets staff reassure your child.

## If no one has arrived 30 minutes after closing
Staff will call every parent and emergency contact on file. If no adult can be reached within an hour of closing, the Director follows the center's child safety procedure, which can include contacting the local authorities.

## Authorized pick-up
Only adults listed on the child's pick-up form may collect a child, and must show photo ID. Changes to the list must be made in writing at the front desk.
