# Late Pickup Policy

Juniper Hill closes at **6:00 PM** for pick-up. Staff schedules and licensing ratios depend on every child leaving on time.

## Fees
- A late fee of **$2.00 per minute** starts at **6:01 PM**. There is no grace period.
- Fees are billed on your next monthly invoice and are due with tuition.
- The time on the front office clock is the official time.

## Repeated lateness
- **3 late pickups in a calendar month:** a written notice from the Assistant Director.
- **5 late pickups in a semester (Aug-Dec or Jan-Jul):** a meeting with the Director, and your child's place may be reviewed.

## If you are running late
Call the front desk at (505) 555-0120 as early as you can. Calling does not remove the fee, but it lets staff reassure your child.

## If no one has arrived by 6:30 PM
Staff will call every parent and emergency contact on file. If no adult can be reached by 7:00 PM, the Director follows the center's child safety procedure, which can include contacting the local authorities.

## Authorized pick-up
Only adults listed on the child's pick-up form may collect a child, and must show photo ID. Changes to the list must be made in writing at the front desk.
